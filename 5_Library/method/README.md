## The through-line

All five notes are versions of one idea: **say what you know, say how you know it, and say what you
don't know.**

That's not modesty. It's what makes an analysis survive contact with a room full of people who have
reasons to disagree with it. The analyses that get taken apart in public are almost never the ones
that admitted uncertainty. They're the ones that hid it and got caught.
