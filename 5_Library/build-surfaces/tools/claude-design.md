# Claude Design

For building interfaces — layout, visual design, multiple screens, real polish.

**What it's for here:** when what you're building is genuinely a product people will use again and
again, and it needs to look like one. Also strong for producing something a design or engineering
team can build from, which is often the realistic path inside a large organization.

**Cost:** included with Claude.

**Quickstart**

1. Describe the interface, who uses it, and what they need to do in it
2. Start from the content — the metrics, the inputs, the decision it supports — not from the visuals
3. Iterate on layout and hierarchy before color and polish
4. Export, or hand off to whoever builds it

**Not for:** a chart and three numbers. That's an artifact, or honestly a spreadsheet.

**The discipline that matters:** design the hierarchy around the decision. The most important number
should be the most visually prominent thing on the screen — and by default it usually isn't, because
tools lay things out in the order you thought of them.
