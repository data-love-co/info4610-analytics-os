## Section 4 — Time Away From Work

### 4.1 Paid Time Off

Regular full-time employees accrue paid time off (PTO) at the following rates:

| Years of service | Accrual per pay period | Annual equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 years | 4.62 hours | 15 days |
| 3–5 years | 6.15 hours | 20 days |
| 6+ years | 7.69 hours | 25 days |

Part-time employees working 20 or more hours per week accrue on a prorated basis. Employees working
fewer than 20 hours per week do not accrue PTO.

PTO must be requested through the HR system at least **10 business days in advance** for requests
of three days or more. Shorter requests require manager approval but no minimum notice.

### 4.2 Carryover

Employees may carry over a maximum of **40 hours** of unused PTO into the following calendar year.
Hours above that cap are forfeited on December 31 and are not paid out.

*(Note added March 2026: an exception process for carryover above the cap exists for employees who
were unable to take leave due to business demands. Requests go to your HR Business Partner. The
approval criteria are under review.)*

### 4.3 Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO and accrues at 1 hour per 30 hours worked, up to 56 hours per year,
in accordance with state law. Unused sick leave carries over without cap but is not paid out at
separation.

Sick leave may be used for the employee's own illness, for the care of a family member, or for
reasons related to domestic violence or sexual assault as defined by applicable state law.

### 4.4 Company Holidays

Eleven paid holidays annually. Employees required to work a company holiday receive holiday pay
plus their regular rate for hours worked. Employees in states with different statutory holiday
requirements should consult their local addendum.

### 4.5 Parental Leave

Birthing parents receive 16 weeks of paid leave. Non-birthing parents and adoptive parents receive
8 weeks of paid leave, to be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement. Leave may be taken
intermittently with manager approval.

Parental leave runs concurrently with FMLA where the employee is FMLA-eligible.

## Section 7 — Remote and Hybrid Work

### 7.1 Eligibility

Remote and hybrid arrangements are determined by role, not by individual request. Roles are
classified as **Onsite**, **Hybrid** (minimum 2 days per week in a company office), or **Remote**.
Your role classification is listed in your offer letter and in the HR system.

Changes to role classification require approval from your manager and your HR Business Partner.

### 7.2 Working From Another State

Employees who wish to work from a state where the company is not registered as an employer must
receive approval before relocating. Approval takes 4 to 6 weeks and is not guaranteed; tax,
payroll, and employment law registration in a new state is a material cost.

Working from another state temporarily — for example, from a family member's home for two weeks —
does not require approval if the total is under 30 days in a calendar year.

### 7.3 Working From Another Country

International remote work requires approval from your HR Business Partner, Legal, and Tax. Assume
a minimum of 8 weeks. Approval is granted rarely and typically only for short, defined periods.

Working from another country without approval — including during personal travel — may create tax
and permanent-establishment exposure for the company and is grounds for corrective action.

### 7.4 Equipment and Home Office

Remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time $750 home office stipend, available after 90 days of
employment, and a company laptop. Monitors, keyboards, and chairs may be purchased against the
stipend. Internet service is not reimbursed.

*(Section 7.4 was updated in the 4.2 revision. An earlier version of this handbook stated the
stipend was $500 and available immediately. The $750/90-day terms are current.)*

### 7.5 Meeting Hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's registered time zone. Meetings
should not be scheduled outside core hours without agreement from all required attendees.
